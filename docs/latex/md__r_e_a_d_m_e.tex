\hypertarget{md__r_e_a_d_m_e_autotoc_md12}{}\doxysection{\texorpdfstring{Scope and Concept}{Scope and Concept}}\label{md__r_e_a_d_m_e_autotoc_md12}
\doxylink{namespace_g_o_a_t}{GOAT} is based on a scene description consisting of optical sources, geometrical objects, and detectors. Ray tracing is performed within this scene, and complex electric field amplitudes are propagated along the rays.

The library deliberately adopts {\bfseries{geometrical optics with explicit phase information}} as its underlying model. Within this framework, interference effects arising from phase differences can be described, whereas phenomena that are inherently wave-\/optical in nature, such as diffraction, lie outside the scope of the model.

\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Scene-\/based and modular architecture (sources, objects, detectors)
\item Ray tracing with propagation of complex electric field amplitudes
\item Explicit phase tracking for the analysis of interference effects
\item Efficient handling of complex geometries, including STL import and octree acceleration
\item Cross-\/platform C++17 implementation (Linux and Windows)
\item Extensive API documentation generated with Doxygen
\end{DoxyItemize}
